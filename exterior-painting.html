Paint Or Replace?

Paint looks best when the siding and trim underneath are sound.

Many Puget Sound homes reach a point where paint alone is not enough. Peeling, swelling, soft trim, failed caulking, or repeated moisture issues may mean the exterior needs repair before the final finish goes on.

Breeze Siding helps homeowners decide whether exterior painting is the right move, or whether siding replacement, trim repair, or window detail work should be handled first so the paint investment lasts longer.

We look for

  • Soft trim or fascia
  • Open joints and failed caulking
  • Peeling paint from moisture issues
  • Siding boards that are cupping or swollen
  • Window and door trim that needs repair before finish
