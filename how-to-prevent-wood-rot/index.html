PNW exterior focus

Wood rot prevention is a system, not a single product.

Northwest homes need good flashing, proper clearances, sound paint, well-sealed penetrations, and siding details that do not hold water. Areas near decks, concrete, rooflines, and windows deserve extra attention.

Practical prevention steps

  • Keep siding and trim above soil, decks, and concrete where possible.
  • Repair failed caulk, peeling paint, and open joints promptly.
  • Check gutters, roof splash, and drainage around the home.
  • Use moisture-ready materials and flashing during replacement work.
