Why It Matters

A window replacement is only as good as the exterior details around it.

In Puget Sound homes, a window opening is one of the most important places to get the siding and trim details right. A clean install should account for flashing, water paths, trim alignment, siding cut lines, and how the finished window looks from the street.

Breeze Siding is useful when window work connects to siding replacement, dry rot repair, paint, or exterior trim. We help homeowners avoid the common problem of a new window surrounded by old, vulnerable details.

Window projects can include

  • Replacement windows
  • Exterior trim replacement
  • Siding tie-ins around openings
  • Flashing and weather review
  • Paint and finish coordination

Good Timing

Replacing windows during siding work can prevent extra work later.

Better flashing access

When siding is already being removed, it is easier to evaluate and improve vulnerable window transitions.

Cleaner trim lines

Window trim can be sized and finished with the new siding instead of patched around later.

Stronger comfort

New windows can help with drafts, comfort, and a more polished finished exterior.
