Fiber cement and vinyl solve different priorities.

Vinyl siding is often chosen for budget and maintenance. Fiber cement is often chosen for curb appeal, durability, paintability, and a more substantial finish. In the Northwest, both still depend on smart flashing and moisture control behind the wall.

Questions to ask

  • How important is a premium architectural finish?
  • Will the home need trim, window, or dry rot repairs at the same time?
  • How exposed is the home to wind-driven rain?
  • Is long-term resale appeal a priority?

Breeze Siding can compare options during the estimate and recommend a direction that fits the home, budget, and neighborhood.
