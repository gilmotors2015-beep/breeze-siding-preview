Moisture control starts behind the siding.

This older dampness topic now points to the updated house wrap and siding replacement guide. It explains why the hidden layers matter as much as the finished siding.

For Northwest homes, the biggest risks often show up around windows, trim, penetrations, lower wall sections, and shaded elevations. A good siding project should address water shedding, drying potential, flashing, and repair needs before new material goes on.
