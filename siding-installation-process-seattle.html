From old siding to finished exterior

This replaces the thin how-to installation article.

Instead of publishing a do-it-yourself installation post that could miss jobsite details, this page explains what a professional siding replacement process should include.

Typical flow

  • Protect the work area
  • Remove old siding
  • Inspect sheathing
  • Install wrap and flashing
  • Set trim and siding

Removal and inspection

Once old siding is removed, the wall can be checked for soft sheathing, old leaks, missing flashing, insect damage, and areas that need repair before new materials cover the wall again.

Water management

House wrap, flashing tape, window details, clearances, and drainage paths are what help the wall shed water. These steps are especially important in the Puget Sound climate.

Trim and siding installation

Trim, corners, starter strips, laps, reveals, and fasteners should be set consistently so the exterior looks intentional and performs as a system.

Finish and cleanup

The final stage includes punch list details, paint or caulk where specified, jobsite cleanup, and a walk-through of the completed work.

A strong siding project is not just new material. It is the whole exterior assembly working together.
