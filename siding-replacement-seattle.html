Why Seattle siding replacement needs careful detailing

Seattle homes deal with wet winters, shaded walls, older construction details, and wind-driven rain. A siding replacement should do more than refresh the exterior; it should protect the wall assembly behind it.

What a Seattle siding contractor should inspect

Before new siding goes up, the condition of the existing siding, trim, sheathing, windows, and flashing should be reviewed. If old water damage is present, it should be addressed before the new exterior is installed.

Material options for Seattle homes

James Hardie fiber cement, vinyl, cedar, and engineered siding can all fit different projects. The right choice depends on budget, maintenance expectations, style, and how the home handles moisture.

Clear estimates and practical scopes

A good Seattle siding estimate should explain removal, prep, repairs, moisture barrier details, trim, siding material, paint or finish, and cleanup so there are fewer surprises once work starts.
