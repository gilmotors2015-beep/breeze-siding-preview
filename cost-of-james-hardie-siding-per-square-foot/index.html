Why square-foot pricing can be misleading.

Two homes with the same wall area can price very differently. A simple rectangular wall is not the same as a home with many windows, second-story access, dry rot repairs, panel accents, or detailed trim transitions.

Cost drivers to expect

  • Lap siding, shingle, board and batten, or panel product choices.
  • Window trim, corner boards, belly bands, and design accents.
  • Old siding removal, disposal, sheathing repair, and house wrap.
  • Access, height, staging, and work around decks or landscaping.

Breeze Siding builds estimates around the actual exterior, so homeowners understand what is included instead of relying on a vague per-square-foot number.

If a quote seems unusually low, ask what happens if damaged sheathing, soft trim, flashing gaps, or water-damaged corners are found after tear-off.

Puget Sound pricing context

A better Hardie estimate starts with the home, not a shortcut number.

James Hardie siding is popular around Seattle and Tacoma because it can handle wet seasons well when installed correctly. The final value comes from the full system: clearances, flashing, trim, water-resistive barrier, caulking approach, and paint-ready finish details.

For homeowners comparing bids, the most useful question is not only “what is the square-foot price?” It is “what is included, what is excluded, and how will the contractor handle the areas where siding usually fails?”

Questions to ask before choosing a bid

  • Does the estimate include trim replacement or just siding?
  • How are window and door flashing details handled?
  • What happens if dry rot is found?
  • Is house wrap or weather barrier work included?
  • Who paints or finishes cut edges and trim details?
