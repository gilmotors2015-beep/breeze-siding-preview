Quick answer: what changes the square-foot price?

For planning, James Hardie and fiber cement siding is often discussed by the square foot, but the final project number depends on much more than wall area. A simple lap siding replacement with clean access is very different from a home with damaged sheathing, window flashing repairs, second-story walls, detailed trim, panel accents, or old siding removal.

Cost drivers to expect

  • Material profile: lap siding, panel, shingle, or board and batten.
  • Tear-off and disposal of existing siding.
  • Trim, corners, window, and door details.
  • Dry rot, sheathing, or framing repairs.
  • House wrap, rainscreen, and drainage details.
  • Access, height, landscaping, staging, paint, and finish details.

Puget Sound pricing context

Why Seattle-Tacoma Hardie projects can price differently.

James Hardie siding is popular around Seattle and Tacoma because it can handle wet seasons well when installed correctly. The final value comes from the full system: clearances, flashing, trim, water-resistive barrier, caulking approach, and paint-ready finish details.

Puget Sound homes often need more than a surface siding swap. Rain exposure, shaded walls, older trim, window transitions, and hidden moisture damage can change the scope after old siding comes off. A good Hardie estimate should explain what is included, what is excluded, and how repair allowances are handled before work begins.

For homeowners comparing bids, the most useful question is not only “what is the square-foot price?” It is “what is included, what is excluded, and how will the contractor handle the areas where siding usually fails?”

Questions to ask before choosing a bid

  • Does the estimate include trim replacement or just siding?
  • How are window and door flashing details handled?
  • What happens if dry rot is found?
  • Is house wrap or weather barrier work included?
  • Who paints or finishes cut edges and trim details?

FAQ

Common James Hardie siding cost questions.

How much does James Hardie siding cost per square foot?

James Hardie siding cost per square foot depends on material profile, old siding removal, trim, repairs, access, weather barrier details, and finish work. A local estimate is more reliable than a broad online range because two homes with the same wall area can require very different scopes.

Is Hardie siding more expensive than vinyl siding?

Hardie siding usually costs more upfront than vinyl siding, but many homeowners choose it for durability, curb appeal, fire resistance, and long-term exterior performance. The better comparison is what each system includes and how it handles Northwest weather.

Why do Hardie siding quotes vary so much?

Quotes vary because contractors may include different scopes. One bid may include trim, flashing, weather barrier, and repair allowances, while another may only include basic siding installation.
