Quick answer: use square-foot pricing for planning, then compare the full scope.

James Hardie siding cost is often discussed per square foot, but two homes with the same wall area can need very different work. A simple lap siding replacement on clean walls is not the same as a project with damaged sheathing, window flashing repairs, second-story access, panel accents, trim replacement, or old siding disposal.

The better question is: what is included in the number? A useful estimate should make the siding system easy to understand, not hide important details behind a single price.

Cost drivers to expect

  • Material profile: lap siding, panel, shingle, or board and batten.
  • Tear-off and disposal of existing siding.
  • Trim, corners, window, and door details.
  • Dry rot, sheathing, or framing repairs.
  • House wrap, flashing, rainscreen, and drainage details.
  • Access, height, landscaping, staging, paint, and finish quality.

For a city-specific scope, start with a Seattle siding replacement estimate or review our siding replacement services to see how the full exterior system fits together.

If a quote seems unusually low, ask whether it includes trim, flashing, disposal, weather barrier work, and what happens if damaged sheathing or dry rot is found after tear-off.

Seattle-Tacoma pricing context

Why local Hardie projects can price differently.

James Hardie siding is popular around Seattle and Tacoma because it can handle wet seasons well when installed correctly. The value comes from the full system: clearances, flashing, trim, water-resistive barrier, caulking approach, and paint-ready finish details.

Puget Sound homes often need more than a surface siding swap. Rain exposure, shaded walls, older trim, window transitions, deck attachments, and hidden moisture damage can change the scope after old siding comes off.

That is why the strongest estimate explains what is included, what is excluded, and how repair discoveries will be handled before work begins.

Questions to ask before choosing a bid

  • Does the estimate include trim replacement or just siding?
  • How are window and door flashing details handled?
  • What happens if dry rot is found?
  • Is house wrap or weather barrier work included?
  • Are disposal, paint, and finish details included?
  • Who handles cut edges, joints, and final cleanup?

What makes one Hardie bid higher or lower than another?

Lower-cost scenarios

Simple wall shapes, clean sheathing, standard lap siding, basic trim, easy access, and straightforward paint scopes usually keep the project simpler.

Higher-cost scenarios

Many windows, damaged sheathing, detailed trim, panel accents, tight side yards, second stories, decks, and rainscreen or flashing upgrades can increase the real scope.

FAQ

Common James Hardie siding cost questions.

How much does James Hardie siding cost per square foot?

It depends on material profile, old siding removal, trim, repairs, access, weather barrier details, and finish work. A local estimate is more reliable than a broad online range because two homes with the same wall area can require very different scopes.

Is Hardie siding more expensive than vinyl siding?

Hardie siding usually costs more upfront than vinyl siding, but many homeowners choose it for durability, curb appeal, fire resistance, and long-term exterior performance. The better comparison is what each system includes and how it handles Northwest weather.

Why do Hardie siding quotes vary so much?

Quotes vary because contractors may include different scopes. One bid may include trim, flashing, weather barrier, disposal, and repair planning, while another may only include basic siding installation.
