Full Exterior Thinking

Most exterior projects touch more than one trade.

A siding replacement may reveal dry rot, failed trim, window issues, paint transitions, or deck details. Planning those pieces together can produce a cleaner finished exterior and fewer surprises.

Breeze Siding keeps the scope practical and clear so homeowners understand what is being repaired, replaced, and finished. That matters in the Pacific Northwest, where rain, shade, and older exterior details can turn small issues into recurring problems.

Homeowners ask us about

  • Siding replacement with fiber cement, lap siding, shingles, panels, or accents
  • Window replacement and flashing corrections
  • Dry rot repair and exterior trim replacement
  • Exterior painting, decks, and finish details
  • Commercial and multifamily siding scopes
