Fiber Cement Siding

Fiber cement siding gives homes a durable, high-end exterior when installed correctly.

The product has real advantages, but the details behind it matter: flashing, clearances, trim, and moisture control all affect long-term performance.

Pros and cons depend on the installation as much as the product.

Fiber cement is popular because it can create a clean, substantial exterior with strong curb appeal. It also accepts paint well and pairs with lap, shingle, board and batten, and panel details. The tradeoff is that it needs proper installation, handling, clearances, and finishing.

Pros

  • Strong premium appearance and broad design flexibility.
  • Durability for wet Northwest conditions when detailed correctly.
  • Works well with updated trim, windows, and modern accents.

Cons

  • Usually costs more than basic vinyl.
  • Requires experienced installation and correct water management.
  • Repairs behind old siding may affect scope.
