What Seattle homes need

A siding estimate should catch the details that fail first.

Seattle siding problems often start around windows, door trim, deck attachments, roof returns, and walls that stay wet or shaded. A quick surface bid can miss the real reason siding is failing. Breeze Siding reviews the exterior as a system, then recommends a scope that balances curb appeal, product durability, and practical repair work.

What siding works well in Seattle?

Fiber cement and James Hardie products are common choices because they handle wet weather well when installed with proper clearances, trim, flashing, and paint details.

Can you inspect dry rot during the estimate?

We can identify visible warning signs during the estimate and talk through likely repair areas. Hidden damage is confirmed as the siding is opened up.

Do you handle windows with siding?

Yes. Window replacement and siding work often belong together because flashing, trim, and weather barrier details need to connect correctly.
