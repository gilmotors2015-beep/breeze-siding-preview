Small clues can reveal a bigger water problem.

Dry rot often begins where water sits, splashes, or sneaks behind trim. Windows, doors, decks, belly bands, and roofline transitions are common starting points.

Watch for these signs

  • Trim that feels soft, punky, or swollen.
  • Paint bubbling, cracking, or peeling in the same area repeatedly.
  • Siding that is wavy, stained, loose, or separating at joints.
  • Musty smell or dampness near window openings.

If you spot these issues, Breeze Siding can inspect the exterior and help you decide whether the repair is localized or tied to a larger siding replacement plan.
