Energy and comfort

Exterior work can improve more than curb appeal.

When old siding comes off, the wall sheathing is accessible. That can make it the practical time to evaluate house wrap, air sealing, and insulation upgrades before everything is covered again.

Consider it when

  • Rooms feel drafty
  • Exterior walls feel cold
  • Old siding is being removed
  • Wall details need rebuilding
  • You want better comfort long term

What insulation under siding can do

Depending on the home and assembly, exterior insulation can help reduce thermal bridging and improve comfort. It should be planned with the correct wall details, siding attachment, trim depth, and moisture management.

Moisture still comes first

Insulation does not replace house wrap, flashing, or good drainage. Any upgrade should work with the exterior wall system so the assembly can shed water and dry appropriately.

Not every project needs it

Some homes benefit more from air sealing, window improvements, attic insulation, or targeted repairs. The right recommendation depends on the condition of the home and the goals of the project.

The best time to talk about insulation is before siding and trim details are finalized.
