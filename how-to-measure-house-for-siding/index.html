A better house measurement is organized by elevation.

Label photos and measurements by front, back, left, and right sides. Add notes for gables, garage walls, decks, chimneys, and covered entries. This gives the estimate a cleaner starting point and makes it easier to spot scope items before the onsite visit.

What not to overlook

  • Garage returns and small walls near entries.
  • Second-story sections over rooflines.
  • Trim profiles around windows and doors.
  • Dry rot, soft fascia, and siding near splash zones.
