Fiber cement

Fiber cement is a strong choice for many Puget Sound homes because it offers a clean painted look, stable performance, and good durability when installed with proper clearances, flashing, and paint finish.

Vinyl siding

Vinyl can be a budget-friendly option for some homes. It is lighter and lower maintenance, but product quality, wall preparation, trim details, and sun exposure all affect the final result.

Metal siding

Metal siding can work well as an accent or modern exterior material. It needs thoughtful detailing around windows, penetrations, and transitions so the finished wall looks intentional and sheds water correctly.

Wood siding

Wood has a classic look, but it asks for more maintenance in the Seattle climate. Paint upkeep, clearances, and ventilation are especially important where rain and shade keep surfaces damp.

The material matters, but the water management behind it matters just as much.
