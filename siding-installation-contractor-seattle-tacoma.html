Hiring a siding installer should be about more than square footage.

Searches for a siding contractor usually start with a simple question: who can install the siding? The better question is who will notice the details that make the project last. Around Seattle and Tacoma, siding installation has to account for rain, shade, old repairs, window openings, deck transitions, low clearances, and the condition of the wall once old siding comes off.

Breeze Siding helps homeowners plan siding installation as an exterior system. That means matching the siding material to the home, reviewing trim and flashing details, checking for dry rot or soft sheathing, and making sure the finished exterior looks intentional from the street.

A good siding installation estimate should explain the scope, product direction, repair assumptions, weather-barrier details, and what happens if hidden damage is found during tear-off.

Contractor Selection

What to look for before choosing a siding contractor.

The right contractor should help you understand what is included, what is excluded, and what might change once the wall is opened. For Puget Sound homes, that includes tear-off, repair allowances, flashing expectations, house wrap, trim details, paint coordination, and cleanup.

If bids look very different, it is often because they are not pricing the same project. One estimate may assume a simple siding swap while another includes trim, flashing, repairs, or more complete weather protection. Breeze Siding works to make those differences easier to understand before work starts.

Useful questions to ask

  • What happens if dry rot or damaged sheathing is found?
  • How are windows, doors, decks, and roof transitions flashed?
  • Which siding material fits the home and maintenance goals?
  • How will trim, paint, cleanup, and project access be handled?

Material guidance

Fiber cement, James Hardie, cedar accents, engineered wood, and panel looks each have different maintenance, cost, and design implications.

Moisture details

House wrap, rainscreen gaps, flashing, and proper clearances matter because Northwest siding has to dry after long wet seasons.

Repair-aware scopes

Soft trim, old patches, water staining, and cracked siding should be discussed before installation begins so surprises are easier to manage.
