What We Handle

Siding replacement is really a full exterior system.

In the Pacific Northwest, siding has to do more than look good. It needs the right clearances, weather barrier, flashing details, rainscreen planning, trim transitions, and product choice for rain, shade, and long damp seasons.

Breeze Siding helps homeowners compare repair versus replacement, decide where James Hardie or fiber cement makes sense, and coordinate windows, paint, trim, or deck details when the project touches more than one part of the exterior.

Common project scopes

  • Full siding tear-off and replacement
  • James Hardie and fiber cement installation
  • Hardie panel siding and modern accents
  • Window trim and flashing coordination
  • Rainscreen and drainage-plane planning for wet or shaded walls
  • Dry rot repair discovered during siding work
