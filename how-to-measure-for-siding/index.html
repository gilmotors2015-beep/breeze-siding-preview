How to measure for siding without missing the details.

Measure the walls, then think about everything that interrupts them: windows, corners, rooflines, penetrations, and trim transitions.

Start with the wall areas.

Measure width times height for each rectangular wall section. Measure gables separately as triangles. If the home has dormers, bay windows, recessed entries, or multiple siding profiles, those areas deserve their own notes.

Photos help more than perfect math

A few clear photos can reveal access, window patterns, electrical blocks, gutters, rooflines, decks, landscaping, and likely moisture-prone areas. Those details shape the final siding plan.

Why Breeze Siding confirms onsite

Puget Sound siding projects often include hidden repair work, flashing improvements, trim replacement, and house wrap details. We use homeowner measurements as a helpful starting point, then verify the scope before pricing.
